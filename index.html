What actually happened above

Every block above started at opacity:0, hidden by elementor-invisible. Nothing changed until the block's own boundary crossed into the viewport — at that point JS swapped in animated, the class our CSS keyframes are keyed on.

The trigger belongs to the element with the animation set — a wrapping section doesn't inherit it from a widget inside it.

By default it plays once. Scrolling back up and down again in a live Elementor page won't replay it — that's why every card here has its own replay control.

Duration and delay come entirely from Elementor's native fields, which is why Widget B above waited an extra 250ms before starting.
